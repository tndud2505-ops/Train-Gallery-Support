Train Gallery Privacy Policy

Publisher: 올라잇소프트
Contact: tndud2503@gmail.com
Effective date: 2026-09-30

Train Gallery is a local-first workout video app. Original videos stay on your device, and the app does not upload videos by default.

Data the app handles

Train Gallery may access camera video, microphone audio when recording with sound, user-selected videos, app-created video assets, and local edit metadata such as capture dates, trim decisions, captions, markers, and calendar grouping.

Data the developer does not collect

  • No account system.
  • No cloud sync.
  • No third-party advertising.
  • No analytics SDK.
  • No location or contact access.
  • No broad gallery scan by default.

Storage

Videos are stored by the operating system in app-created or user-selected locations on the device. Train Gallery stores edit metadata in an on-device SQLite database.

Sharing

When you use sharing controls, Train Gallery opens the operating system share sheet for the selected local video. The app does not choose the destination app or upload the video to the developer.

Deletion

Remove from app deletes the workout entry, captions, trim decisions, markers, and calendar grouping metadata from Train Gallery. Remove from app does not delete the original video from your device photo library or media library.

Future changes

If future versions add accounts, cloud upload, analytics, crash reporting, payments, subscriptions, coaching, or social features, this policy and the store disclosures must be updated before release.
