Video storage

The app does not upload videos by default. Original videos stay on your device unless you choose to share or move them through operating system tools.

Removing entries

Remove from app does not delete the original video. It removes the local Train Gallery entry and related edit metadata such as captions, trims, markers, and calendar grouping.

Exports

Exported workout clips are generated locally and can be saved or shared by user action. If an export fails, check available storage and try a shorter clip.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy for details about local-first video handling and permissions.
